# Business Profitability & Inventory Analysis

## Case study
Northstar Office Supplies is a simulated multichannel office-products retailer. The dataset was intentionally designed for this portfolio case study, with realistic operational relationships across sales, discounts, costs, shipping, product categories, stock levels, reorder points, suppliers, and warehouses.

## Business questions
1. Which products, categories, regions, and customer segments generate profit rather than merely sales?
2. Where do discounts erode profitability?
3. Which products carry an immediate stockout risk?
4. Which product lines combine strong demand with weak inventory coverage?
5. What actions should management prioritize for the next buying cycle?

## Raw data
- data/raw/orders.csv: 2,400 order lines from 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31.
- data/raw/products.csv: product-level master data and unit costs.
- data/raw/inventory_snapshot.csv: end-of-period inventory snapshot.
- data_dictionary.csv: definitions and expected data types.

## Important transparency note
This is a simulated dataset, not a claim about an external company. All calculations, findings, assumptions, and limitations will be documented in the final case study.
